Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FENS Trade FZ-LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Benim Deponum mobile application (the "App"), available on Android and iOS. By creating an account or otherwise using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide to us

CategoryExamples
Account & registration data Username, email address, password, first and last name, phone number, country/country code, preferred language
Profile data Any details you add or edit in your profile
Listing content Product photos, titles, descriptions, categories, attributes, and other details you submit when creating or editing a listing
Photos & media Images you choose to upload from your camera or photo library when creating a listing (the App only accesses images you explicitly select)
Support communications Information you provide when you contact us for support

1.2 Information collected automatically

CategoryExamples
Push notification data A device push token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver notifications about your listings and account activity
Technical & usage data App version, device type/OS, and basic diagnostic information necessary to operate the service and push notifications
We do not collect your precise or approximate device location. The App does not request GPS/location permissions — any city or region field you see is text you type in yourself.
We do not process payments. The App does not include in-app purchases or payment processing, so we do not collect card numbers, bank details, or other financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your identity (e.g., email verification codes)
  • To let you create, publish, and manage product listings
  • To send push and in-app notifications (e.g., activity on your listings, moderation updates, account notices)
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the App
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Other users: Information you include in a public listing (e.g., product photos, description, and your seller profile details as displayed in the App) is visible to other users of the App.
  • Service providers: We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications, which involves sharing a device token with Google. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Hosting/infrastructure: Your account and listing data is stored on our backend infrastructure, which we operate to provide the App's core functionality.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and listing data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the App's services. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain information to comply with legal, tax, accounting, or security obligations, or to resolve disputes.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data protection law (such as the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data directly in the App: go to More → Delete Account and confirm. You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

Managing notifications

You can control push notification permissions at any time from your device's system settings, and manage in-app notification preferences from the App's notification settings screen.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) between the App and our servers and password hashing. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

We are based in the United Arab Emirates, and your information may be stored and processed in the UAE or other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may contain links to third-party services (such as Google Firebase). This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
